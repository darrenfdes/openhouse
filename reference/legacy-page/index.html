Velsao House: three ways to start

The owner won't pay to finish the building or cut the rent, so every plan pays ₹80k a month, rent-free only during the 6-month fit-out.

  • Plan A, full fit-out: Dulce's ₹35 L construction plus a 20% buffer and good furnishing, 6 rooms with café and co-working. About ₹1.02 Cr in total.
  • Plan B, ₹40 L lean: the same 6 rooms, café and co-working on a ₹40 L build budget, plus deposit and buffer. About ₹50 L in total.
  • Plan C, ₹20 L rooms only: 5 rooms (3 new, 2 heritage), no kitchen, café, breakfast or co-working. ₹20 L all-in, including deposit and buffer.

All figures are in ₹ lakh (1 L = ₹1,00,000) unless marked.

Profit and loss over the lease

Rooms GST (5% at or below ₹7,500 a night) is charged on top and excluded. Tax is 31.2% for the LLP, after spreading the fit-out cost over 9 years. Costs rise 6% a year and pay 7% a year. In year 9 the deposit and working capital come back.

Investment, side by side

Before you rely on ₹40 L

  • Construction in Plan B is ₹22 L, ₹13 L below Dulce's ₹35 L. Ask her what ₹22 L buys. Then decide what to cut: keep the sheet roof and insulate it, re-use floors where they're sound, and leave the false ceilings out.
  • The lean rooms get about ₹1.4 L each: an AC, a good bed, simple storage, linen and lights. Check that they still justify ₹5,000–7,500 a night in photos.
  • There's no contingency inside the ₹40 L. Old Goan houses hide termite damage, damp in laterite walls and drainage problems, so plan for a 25% overrun and use the slider.
  • Get two contractor quotes for a fixed price. Hold back 10% until snagging is complete.
  • Everything you build stays with the owner. Get the registered 9-year lease and lock-in signed before any work starts.
  • Plan C's ₹5.5 L of construction only covers finishing work: paint, tiling patches, plumbing and wiring for 5 rooms. It can't wall and roof the upper floor, so the 3 new rooms must fit on the ground floor of the new section, or the plan fails.

How to use the two plans with the owner

Opening position

  • You're adding ₹22–42 L of permanent value to the owner's house at ₹80k rent. Still ask for something in return: 3 extra rent-free months, or rent flat for 5 years instead of 3.
  • Start with Plan B. It needs half of Plan A's cash and earns the most per rupee. Plan C needs the least cash but has to fill far more of its rooms.
  • Either way: a 9-year registered lease, the owner locked in for 5 years or more, a commercial-use NOC, and written consent for the construction works.

Walk away if

  • Rent goes above ₹85k a month.
  • Itemised quotes for the Plan B scope come in above ₹50 L, which is 25% over the ₹40 L.
  • There's no registered lease with the commercial-use NOC, or the owner can end the lease before year 5.

Market check

  • South Goa listings: Majorda averages ₹6,129 a night at 32% occupancy, Benaulim ₹6,397 at 38%, Colva ₹6,502 at 35% (AirROI, trailing 12 months). These are per listing, and many listings are whole villas.
  • Goa's listings rose 66% in a year to 11,259, while the average rate stayed flat (Airbtics). Foreign arrivals are about 45% below 2017.
  • Heritage stays in the south sell at a premium: Silva Heritage in Benaulim charges about ₹8,200 with breakfast. The Velsao–Majorda belt has no co-working.
